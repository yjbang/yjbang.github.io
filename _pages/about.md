I am a Ph.D. Candidate at Center for AI Research ([CAiRE](https://caire.hkust.edu.hk/)), The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology ([HKUST](https://hkust.edu.hk/)).

My research focuses on LLM evaluation, value alignment, responsible AI, and safety in AI systems.

Honored with the Area Chair Award at AACL 2023, my [contributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04023) have earned a spot among the [Top 100 AI achievements (2022-2023)](https://www.benchcouncil.org/evaluation/ai/annual.html). I serve as a reviewer for top-tier NLP conferences such as ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, ARR and Harvard Data Science Review.

I am currently seeking full-time opportunities.
